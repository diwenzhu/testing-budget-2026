Reading this

  • A win costs about $3,271, not $880. A single winning test only burns $880.19, but you fund every test to get one — so plan against the true figure. The gap is what learning costs.
  • New Programs convert better than Localizations (28.6% vs 21.4%), and cost less per win ($2,846 vs $3,839) — despite near-identical spend per test. The two types cost the same to run; they do not pay back the same.
  • $6,081 went to Cancelled tests (26.6% of all spend). Killing weak tests sooner is the cheapest available saving.
  • These rates will move. 7 tests are Iterating and 5 Monitoring — none have a final verdict yet. Success rate is a floor, not a settled figure.
